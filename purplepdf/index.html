Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to download anything?

No. Every tool listed here is a web application. You simply click the card, and the tool opens in your browser ready to use.

Is it free?

Yes, all tools discovered and listed by PurplePDF are free to use.

Are my files secure?

Most tools listed process files entirely within your browser (client-side), meaning your sensitive data never leaves your device. Always check the individual tool's privacy policy for verification.

Can I use this on mobile?

Absolutely. Since these are web-based tools, they are compatible with smartphones and tablets, provided you have a modern web browser installed.

Are there file size limits?

This depends on your browser's memory and the specific tool you are using. Generally, client-side tools can handle documents up to several hundred megabytes without issues.
